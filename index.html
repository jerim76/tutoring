Ask a Question
Are lessons one-on-one?

Yes. Excel Tuition Centre focuses on personalised one-on-one online tuition, with the tutor working directly with the individual learner.

Who can benefit from extra tuition?

Students may benefit if they need additional explanation, subject-specific support, examination preparation, revision or a more structured learning routine.

Do you support international curricula?

Yes. Tuition can support Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, IB, OxfordAQA International, AQA, OCR and WJEC / Eduqas pathways.

Do you support IB?

Yes. Support is available across the IB PYP, MYP, DP and CP programmes, depending on the subject and learner's needs.

Do you prepare students for SAT and IELTS?

Yes. We provide preparation support for SAT, IELTS, TOEFL and ACT.

Do you provide homeschooling support?

Yes. Homeschooling families can enquire about personalised online academic support based on the learner's programme.

How can I make an enquiry?

You can call us, contact us through WhatsApp, send an email, or complete the enquiry form on this page.
